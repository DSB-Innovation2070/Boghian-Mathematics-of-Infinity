# Mathematics of Infinity: The Boghian Model

> **The official repository for the Daniel Silviu Boghian Model: A new paradigm in pure mathematics defining the closed-loop convergence of infinity and the n * n' = 1 symmetry.**

---

### 1. The Core Innovation
This model, developed by **Daniel Silviu Boghian**, proposes a fundamental shift in how we perceive limits and numerical expansion. By treating infinity not as a divergent "endless line," but as a **convergent point of collapse**, we stabilize mathematical systems that were previously considered "undefined" or "error-prone."

### 2. Key Mathematical Pillars
* **The Inversion Formula:** `n * n' = 1`. This ensures that for every action in the real plane (n), there is a balancing reaction in the mirror plane (n').
* **The Singularity Postulate:** `LIM (n → ∞) = 0s`. This formula proves that the highest possible expansion is mathematically identical to the absolute point of origin.
* **The Activation Engine:** Focusing on the `[1, 2]` interval as the primary pulse of numerical growth.

### 3. Practical Applications
The Boghian Mathematics can be applied in:
* **Quantum Computing:** Preventing data overflow in infinite probability simulations.
* **Theoretical Physics:** Explaining the toroidal flow of energy and matter.
